Subject: What should the FLaNN community read? Help build our Tasting Menu

There are more papers than any of us can read carefully. Three times a year, the FLaNN Tasting Menu will highlight papers that people in our community feel strongly about.

For this edition, recommend papers from [PAPER WINDOW / CONFERENCE CYCLE]. The call runs from [OPENING DATE, TIME, TIME ZONE] to [CLOSING DATE, TIME, TIME ZONE].

Nominate a paper or support an existing nomination: [TASTING MENU URL]

Choose a recommendation (+1) or an enthusiastic endorsement (+5), then write at least a line answering: “Why do you believe this is a good paper?” Tell us in your own words. What makes you excited about it, or what could its implications be? No prescribed review format is required.

Papers reaching 20 points qualify after hosts check eligibility, duplicates, conflicts, and foul play. Four +5 endorsements, twenty +1 recommendations, or a mixture can get there. We aim for roughly five papers, but every paper meeting the threshold qualifies; there is no top-five cutoff.

Please recommend work you have genuinely spent time with. Self-nominations are welcome: declare your relationship, and author/coauthor or other conflicted recommendations count at most +1. One person has one counted position per paper. A GitHub account is needed; reactions do not count as votes.

Your name, relationship, and recommendation are public. By contributing, you agree that FLaNN may quote your words with attribution and a link. Hosts will publish validation adjustments transparently. All valid nominations remain available; being below the threshold is not a negative judgment about a paper.

After results are public, qualifying authors may be invited to contribute a written guide or video. A highlight can also stand on the community's recommendations alone.
