Hey everyone! There are more papers each cycle than any of us can read carefully, and it’s easy to miss something that someone else found exciting.

We’re putting together a **FLaNN Tasting Menu**: three editions a year highlighting papers that people in this community want others to discover. The idea is to make those recommendations public, along with what makes the work worth reading.

Here’s how it would work:

- Suggest a paper you’ve spent time with, or support an existing nomination on GitHub.
- Give it a **+1 recommendation** or a **+5 enthusiastic endorsement**, with at least a line about why you believe it’s good. Write freely: what excites you, or what could its implications be?
- Papers reaching **20 points** qualify after hosts check the public results for eligibility, conflicts, and foul play. Four enthusiastic readers, twenty recommendations, or a mixture can get a paper there.

Self-nominations are welcome and count as +1. Recommendations are public, and highlights will feature readers’ comments with attribution. We’ll also invite authors to contribute an optional written or video guide.

We’ll share the first round’s dates, paper window, and submission link soon. In the meantime, start thinking about a paper you’d love more people to read!
