How it works

Recommend a paper and tell us why it matters. After voting, hosts check the results and invite authors to contribute a short guide or video.

Recommendations and endorsements

A recommendation counts as +1; an enthusiastic endorsement counts as +5. Both need a written reason. Each person has one counted contribution per paper. Authors and other declared conflicts count as +1. Read the voting rules →

What happens after voting?

Hosts check duplicates, conflicts, and reported irregularities, and explain any corrections. Authors can contribute a guide or video; the community’s comments can also stand on their own.

How are comments used?

Comments may be quoted with the recommender’s name, relationship to the paper, and a link to the original public contribution.
